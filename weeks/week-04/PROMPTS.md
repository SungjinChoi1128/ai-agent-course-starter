# Week 4 copy-paste prompts

## Turn goals into checks

```text
Read my brief and current app. Propose three manual checks a non-developer can perform.
Each check must name the action and the observable result. Do not edit the app yet.
```

## Minimal fix from evidence

```text
Expected: [EXPECTED]. Actual: [ACTUAL]. Steps: [STEPS].
Find the smallest fix. Do not rebuild from scratch. Then tell me how to recheck this and one previously working path.
```

## Regression pass

```text
I will re-run these checks: [LIST]. Watch with me and help me record pass/fail.
If something fails, suggest one discriminating check before any new feature work.
```
