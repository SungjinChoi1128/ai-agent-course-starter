# Week 4 exercises

Work in your own project under `workspace/projects/`. Use synthetic data only. Copy blank worksheets from [templates](../../templates/README.md).

## Hands-on exercise

1. Restart the project and record the baseline check in an inspection log.
2. Watch a peer use it without instruction; record three observations.
3. Separate defects, preferences and future features.
4. Choose one observation and write expected versus actual plus a preserve-list.
5. Ask the agent for the smallest change that resolves it.
6. Repeat the original action and capture the after result.
7. Choose a second small improvement and repeat.
8. Run the baseline and both new checks; record pass, fail or not tested.

## Minimum completion

Two inspection-log entries with before/after evidence and a baseline recheck. One completed improvement is acceptable if the second revealed a well-recorded blocker.

## Stretch exercise

Try keyboard-only use or a narrow window if your project has a UI. Describe the actual obstacle before asking for an improvement.

## Homework: 20–30 minutes

Ask someone outside the class to attempt one task using fake data. Record one surprise and make at most one change based on that observation.

## Submit or show

Two bounded changes, a before/after log and an explicit list of deferred ideas.

You may demonstrate live or share a sanitized screenshot and short note. Do not upload raw chats, real credentials or private work materials.
