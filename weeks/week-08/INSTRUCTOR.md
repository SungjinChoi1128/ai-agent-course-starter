# Week 8 instructor notes

## Preparation

Timebox ruthlessly. Prepare a speaking order. Remind learners: silent peer test is the bar. Keep secrets and client data out of demos.

## Open

“Today you own the outcome. If a peer cannot run it while you stay quiet, the handover is not done.”

## Facilitation

- Cut features that threaten the demo.
- During peer test, authors may only answer if the peer is fully stuck for more than a minute — then fix the handover, not just whisper the click.
- Celebrate honest limitations.

## Debrief

Collect handovers and one limitation each. Course complete when peers can run the demos silent.
