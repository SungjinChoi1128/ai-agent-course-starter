# Week 8 copy-paste prompts

## Freeze scope

```text
Here is my capstone idea: [IDEA]. Prior pieces: [WHAT EXISTS].
Help me cut this to a 15-minute demo with three checks, one edge case, and explicit non-goals.
Do not add new services. Wait for approval before editing.
```

## Finish for demo

```text
Implement only what is required for the approved acceptance checks.
Preserve working behavior. When done, draft handover steps a peer can follow with me silent.
```

## Peer-test repair

```text
Peer attempt notes: [CONFUSION / FAILURES].
Fix the handover and the smallest product issue that blocked them.
Then list the exact steps I should re-run to verify.
```
