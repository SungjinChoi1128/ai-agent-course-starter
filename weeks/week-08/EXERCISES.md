# Week 8 exercises

Work in your own project under `workspace/projects/`. Use synthetic data only. Copy blank worksheets from [templates](../../templates/README.md).

## Hands-on exercise

1. Choose one bounded capstone from the ideas sheet or continue your existing project.
2. Complete a brief with the intended user, example data, non-goals, three success checks and one failure case.
3. Agree the brief with the instructor before building.
4. Build or finish the smallest version in the project folder.
5. Have a peer attempt it using only your instructions.
6. Correct the highest-impact issue and rerun the original checks.
7. Write the handover with start/stop steps, data sources, limitations and verification evidence.
8. Deliver a five-minute demonstration. Publish only if you separately choose to and have reviewed the exact contents and destination.

## Minimum completion

A usable local artifact, three recorded checks, one edge-case check and a handover a peer can follow. If incomplete, submit actual working scope and a precise blocker; do not label it complete.

## Stretch exercise

After the course, publish a reviewed copy to a repository you own or add one already-understood integration. Treat publication and scheduling as separately authorised actions.

## Homework: 20–30 minutes

After graduation, use the project for one week with appropriate non-sensitive data. Record one genuine friction point and repeat the interview/build/inspect/improve loop for one small change.

## Submit or show

Capstone brief, project, inspection evidence, runbook, handover and a next-step note.

You may demonstrate live or share a sanitized screenshot and short note. Do not upload raw chats, real credentials or private work materials.
