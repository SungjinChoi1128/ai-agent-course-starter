# Week 8: Capstone: own a useful result

Define, finish, verify, demonstrate and hand over. 90 minutes, three learners.

## Expected outcomes

- Deliver one bounded project that helps a specific user.
- Demonstrate three success checks and one failure or edge case.
- Give a peer enough instructions to use and restart it, with honest limitations.

## Before class

Students arrive with a small idea, their current project and a draft brief. Use materials/capstone-ideas.md and templates/handover.md. New integrations are out of scope unless already working.

## 90-minute agenda

| Time | Minutes | Activity | What learners do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10 | 10 | Pitch and scope | Each learner gives a one-minute user/problem pitch; agree the smallest result. |
| 10–20 | 10 | Acceptance contract | Finalise three success checks, one edge case and explicit non-goals. |
| 20–45 | 25 | Build or finish | Implement the remaining bounded work with a halfway checkpoint. |
| 45–60 | 15 | Peer test | Rotate five-minute user tests; authors record observations. |
| 60–72 | 12 | Correct and verify | Resolve the highest-impact issue and rerun acceptance checks. |
| 72–87 | 15 | Demos | Three five-minute demos: problem, result, evidence and limitation. |
| 87–90 | 3 | Handover and next step | Save the handover and name one next improvement. |
