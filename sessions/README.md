# Sessions

Records of the live sessions, one folder per session, named `YYYY-MM-DD-topic`.

Each folder holds:

- `notes.md` - summary, analysis, notable quotes and takeaways
- `transcript.md` - the full diarised transcript, verbatim

Transcripts are produced automatically (Whisper `large-v3-turbo` with word-level
timestamps, pyannote 3.1 for speaker diarisation), so expect mishearings of names
and jargon. They are left uncorrected rather than tidied, so the record matches
what was actually said.

Names of people who were not in the call have been replaced.
